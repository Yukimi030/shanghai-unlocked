Payment

This is the number one concern for first-time visitors. Here's the truth:

Credit cards: Visa and Mastercard work at hotels and upscale restaurants, but about 70% of local spots — street food, neighborhood restaurants — only accept mobile QR code payment. Your card will be declined without warning.

The solution — Alipay:

  • Download the Alipay International app.
  • Link your Visa or Mastercard directly — no Chinese bank account required.
  • Scan QR codes everywhere, just like locals.

Getting Around

Correct — Uber does not operate in China. Here is what works:

  • DiDi Greater China — China's Uber. The international version accepts foreign cards and has a full English interface. Download and set up before your flight.
  • Shanghai Metro — Excellent coverage and English signage. Pay directly via Alipay's built-in transit QR code.

⚠️ At airports: ignore anyone offering a ride. Use DiDi or the official taxi queue. Never get into an unmarked vehicle.

✅ On our companion days, all transport is handled — private car, driver, navigation.

  • Apple Maps — iPhone users, you're in luck. Apple Maps works perfectly in China with live navigation and English labels. Download offline maps for Shanghai before you land.
  • Amap (Gaode) — More accurate for local spots. Has a working English interface. Top recommendation for Android users.

⚠️ Do not rely on a VPN to use Google Maps. Google Maps uses outdated satellite data for China and is less accurate than local apps.

Internet & VPN

Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, and all Google services are blocked in China.

During your companion day with us: we provide a mobile Wi-Fi device throughout the day at no extra charge.

For your independent time:

  • eSIM (recommended) — Purchase a China Unicom Hong Kong eSIM before departure. Activates instantly and lets you use local apps without needing a VPN.
  • VPN — Must be purchased and installed before you land. ExpressVPN, NordVPN, and Astrill are reliable options. VPNs sometimes disconnect, so do not rely on VPN for navigation or payments.

Visa Requirements

For US Citizens: You generally need to apply in advance for a Chinese Tourist Visa (L Visa). However, if you are transiting through Shanghai to a third country (not returning directly to the US), you may qualify for a 144-hour visa-free transit.

For UK / EU / Australia Citizens: China has significantly expanded visa-free access. As of 2026, citizens of most EU countries, the UK, Australia, and New Zealand can enter visa-free for 30 days.

⚠️ Visa policy changes frequently. Always check the latest information with the Chinese Embassy or Consulate in your country before booking travel.

Safety

Shanghai is one of the safest major cities in the world. There is almost no violent crime against tourists.

What to actually watch for:

  • Tourist scams — "Tea ceremony" invitations or overpriced trinket shops near The Bund. Annoying but not dangerous. Ignore and walk away.
  • Digital isolation — The real risk for most visitors is being unable to navigate, pay, or communicate in an emergency. This is exactly what our pre-arrival setup solves.

✅ Solo female travelers consistently rate Shanghai among their most comfortable solo destinations. Public spaces are well-lit, and the metro is busy and safe at all hours.
