Shanghai City Sets

Three Curated Day Plans — Or Build Your Own

Choose one of three curated day plans — or build your own (see below). Want to swap something? Go ahead — any spot in any set can be replaced with another of similar duration. Same rules as Build Your Own Day apply.

Set A — Classic Shanghai

Best for first-timers. The essential Shanghai, from tree-lined lanes to ancient gardens to the skyline.

First-Timer Pick
TimeWhatDuration
9:00 – 12:30 French Concession Walk — Jing'an Temple (exterior) → Wukang Road → Anfu Road → Julu / Fumin / Changle Roads 3.5 hrs
12:30 – 13:30 Lunch — choose from our recommended restaurants near Jing'an / French Concession 1 hr
13:30 – 15:30 Yu Garden (豫园) — Ming Dynasty garden, rockeries, pavilions, and the bustling bazaar around it 2 hrs
15:30 – 16:30 The Bund (外滩) — the iconic skyline walk, timed for golden hour 1 hr
17:00 Drop-off at your dinner spot
Walking: High

Wear comfortable shoes. On Mondays Yu Garden is closed — we'll swap in Shanghai Museum or Shanghai Tower.

Set B — Chinese Heritage

For culture lovers. Temples, propaganda art, and a 1,700-year-old pagoda next to contemporary art galleries.

Culture Pick
TimeWhatDuration
9:00 – 10:30 Jade Buddha Temple (玉佛寺) — a living monastery housing two exquisite jade Buddha statues from Myanmar 1.5 hrs
10:50 – 11:50 Propaganda Poster Art Centre — a hidden-gem museum of original Chinese propaganda posters (1920s–1990s). TripAdvisor 4.5★, Travelers' Choice 2025 1 hr
12:00 – 13:00 Lunch — near Changning / Xuhui area 1 hr
13:30 – 17:00 Longhua Temple + West Bund — Shanghai's oldest temple (1,700 yrs) with its ancient pagoda, then cross into the West Bund for contemporary art museums and riverside strolls 3.5 hrs
17:30 Drop-off at your dinner spot
Walking: Medium

The contrast between the ancient Longhua Pagoda and the avant-garde West Bund is what makes this day special. The Propaganda Poster Art Centre is notoriously hard to find (7th floor of an office building) — but we know the way.

Set C — Easy & Comfort

Museum, design district, and the Bund — all at a relaxed pace. Great for rainy days, families, or anyone who wants less walking and more air-conditioning.

Relaxed Pick
TimeWhatDuration
9:00 – 11:00 Shanghai Museum (上海博物馆) — world-class collection of Chinese art and artifacts. Free entry, indoor, TripAdvisor 4.6★ 2 hrs
11:30 – 13:00 Columbia Circle / 上生新所 + Lunch — a 1920s expat club turned design district. Tiffany-blue pool, Sun Ke Villa by legendary architect László Hudec, Tsutaya Books, and multiple restaurants on site 1.5 hrs
13:30 – 14:30 Nanjing Road West (南京西路) — the first half of Shanghai's most famous shopping street. Historic architecture, not the full tourist strip 1 hr
14:30 – 15:30 The Bund (外滩) — skyline, river breeze, photos 1 hr
16:00 Optional: afternoon tea at Yuangu (元古) or Peninsula Hotel
Walking: Low

Mostly indoors. If you'd rather sip tea in a courtyard than pound the pavement, this is your set.

Special Events & Notes

Good to Know Before You Go

ItemHeads Up
🏮 Marriage Market Weekends only, 1–5 PM, People's Park. Weather-dependent. If it's raining, skip it.
👑 Gongyan (宫宴) Immersive imperial banquet — hanfu dress-up, live performances, seven courses. ¥398–798/person. Total duration: ~3 hours (including costume change + dinner + show), plus arriving 1.5–2 hrs early if you want makeup done. Must book 15–30 days in advance via WeChat — we can help reserve. Note: choosing Gongyan as lunch means you'll lose most of the afternoon; it's best as a standalone dinner experience.
