What's Included

Private car + driver, English-speaking companion. We cover the cost of food for our driver and companion throughout the day.

Not Included

Attraction tickets, optional English guide fees (pay on-site if you choose to use one), personal expenses, your lunch and dinner.

Dinner: We'll recommend restaurants and can help book a table. We'll drop you off and leave you to enjoy — unless you invite us to join. If you choose to treat your companion to dinner, that's entirely up to you.

Fine Print

Booking: DM us on Instagram / TikTok, or email. We'll confirm within 24 hours.

Payment: PayPal. Link shared upon booking confirmation.

Cancellation: Full refund up to 48 hours before. 50% refund 24–48 hours. No refund within 24 hours.

Overtime: ¥300 per hour, rounded up. If we're having a great time and you want to extend — just let us know.

Custom requests: Anything you want to add, change, or combine — tell us. We'll figure it out and quote case by case.

Note: We are local companions, not licensed tour guides. For historical sites that require certified guide commentary (e.g. Yu Garden, Jade Buddha Temple), we can arrange an English-speaking licensed guide — optional, pay on-site. Or explore with us using the on-site English signage.

Liability: We're your companions, not a travel agency. We'll take every care, but you're responsible for your own travel insurance, valuables, and personal safety. Third-party services (licensed guides, restaurants, venues) operate under their own terms.
