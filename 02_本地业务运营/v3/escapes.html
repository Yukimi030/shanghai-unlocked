Escapes

Out of the City,
Into Another China

Full-day escapes from Shanghai. Water towns, ancient canals, UNESCO gardens — all within an hour or two.

Panlong Tiandi (蟠龙天地)

~1 hr driveFree entryMetro-accessible

A 1,400-year-old water town reborn — canals, rowing boats (摇橹船), contemporary art, riverside dining. Ancient bones, modern soul.

Zhujiajiao (朱家角)

~1 hr driveClassic water town

The classic Jiangnan water town — stone bridges, narrow lanes, boat rides through ancient canals. The Shanghai that existed long before skyscrapers.

Suzhou (苏州)

~1.5 hr driveUNESCO gardensGrand Canal

UNESCO gardens, Grand Canal, silk culture. A full-day immersion in classical China — gardens that inspired poets, and canals that built an empire.
