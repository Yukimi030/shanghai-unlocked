Shanghai Routes

Three Curated Days.
Pick One, Make It Yours.

Each route is a different side of Shanghai. Want to swap something? Go ahead — any spot can be replaced with another of similar duration.

Set A

Classic Shanghai

Best for first-timers. The essential Shanghai, from tree-lined lanes to ancient gardens to the skyline.

9:00 – 12:30

French Concession Walk

Jing'an Temple (exterior) → Wukang Road → Anfu Road → Julu / Fumin / Changle Roads. Tree-lined streets, lane houses, and the soul of old Shanghai.

12:30 – 13:30

Lunch

Choose from our recommended restaurants near Jing'an or the French Concession.

13:30 – 15:30

Yu Garden (豫园)

Ming Dynasty garden, rockeries, pavilions, and the bustling bazaar around it. English guide available on-site — optional, pay on-site.

15:30 – 16:30

The Bund (外滩)

The iconic skyline walk, timed for golden hour.

17:00

Drop-off at your dinner spot

Walking level: High. Wear comfortable shoes. On Mondays Yu Garden is closed — we'll swap in Shanghai Museum or Shanghai Tower.
Set B

Chinese Heritage

For culture lovers. Temples, propaganda art, and a 1,700-year-old pagoda next to contemporary art galleries.

9:00 – 10:30

Jade Buddha Temple (玉佛寺)

A living monastery housing two exquisite jade Buddha statues from Myanmar. English guide available on-site — optional, pay on-site.

10:50 – 11:50

Propaganda Poster Art Centre

A hidden-gem museum of original Chinese propaganda posters (1920s–1990s). TripAdvisor 4.5★, Travelers' Choice 2025.

12:00 – 13:00

Lunch

Near Changning / Xuhui area.

13:30 – 17:00

Longhua Temple + West Bund

Shanghai's oldest temple (1,700 yrs) with its ancient pagoda, then cross into the West Bund for contemporary art museums and riverside strolls.

17:30

Drop-off at your dinner spot

Walking level: Medium. The contrast between the ancient Longhua Pagoda and the avant-garde West Bund is what makes this day special. The Propaganda Poster Art Centre is on the 7th floor of an office building — but we know the way.
Set C

Easy & Comfort

Museum, design district, and the Bund — all at a relaxed pace. Great for rainy days, families, or anyone who wants less walking and more air-conditioning.

9:00 – 11:00

Shanghai Museum (上海博物馆)

World-class collection of Chinese art and artifacts. Free entry, indoor, TripAdvisor 4.6★.

11:30 – 13:00

Columbia Circle / 上生新所 + Lunch

A 1920s expat club turned design district. Tiffany-blue pool, Sun Ke Villa by legendary architect Laszlo Hudec, Tsutaya Books, and multiple restaurants on site.

13:30 – 14:30

Nanjing Road West (南京西路)

The first half of Shanghai's most famous shopping street. Historic architecture.

14:30 – 15:30

The Bund (外滩)

Skyline, river breeze, photos.

16:00

Optional: afternoon tea

At Yuangu (元古) or Peninsula Hotel.

Walking level: Low. Mostly indoors. If you'd rather sip tea in a courtyard than pound the pavement, this is your set.
