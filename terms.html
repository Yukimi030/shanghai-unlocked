Booking Terms

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

1. Our Service

Shanghai Unlocked provides a private, full-day companion experience in Shanghai. Our service includes:

  • An English-speaking local companion for approximately 8 hours
  • Private car and driver for the duration of your experience
  • Real-time translation, navigation, and logistical support
  • A mobile Wi-Fi device for the day at no extra charge
  • A pre-arrival instructional video sent after booking confirmation

Our service does not include attraction tickets, meals, or personal shopping expenses unless explicitly stated otherwise in your booking confirmation.

2. Booking and Deposit

To reserve your date, you must:

  • Submit a booking request through our website form
  • Pay a non-refundable deposit of $100 USD via PayPal
  • Receive a written confirmation email from Shanghai Unlocked

Your booking is not confirmed until we send you a confirmation email. We aim to respond to all booking requests within 24 hours.

3. Payment

Deposit: $100 USD due at the time of booking to secure your date.

Balance Payment: The remaining balance is due and payable at least 24 hours before your Service Date. Where agreed in writing by Shanghai Unlocked, payment may be made on the Service Date prior to the commencement of the service.

Pay-on-Behalf: During your experience, your companion may cover incidental payments (such as attraction tickets or small purchases) on your behalf. All such payments must be settled in full at the end of the day, calculated on the actual RMB total paid. We provide a clear itemized summary of all pay-on-behalf transactions.

Accepted payment methods: PayPal (deposit), cash (RMB or USD), Alipay, and WeChat Pay (balance and pay-on-behalf settlement).

4. Cancellation and Refunds

We understand that plans change. Our cancellation policy is as follows:

  • 7 or more days before your scheduled date — full refund of deposit
  • 3 to 6 days before your scheduled date — 50% refund of deposit
  • Less than 72 hours before your scheduled date — deposit is non-refundable
  • No-show — full deposit forfeited

If Shanghai Unlocked must cancel your booking for any reason, you will receive a full refund of your deposit and any pre-paid balance within 7 business days.

To cancel, email shanghaiunlocked@gmail.com. The cancellation effective date is the date we receive your email.

5. Changes to Your Booking

If you need to change your date, please contact us as soon as possible. We will accommodate date changes at no extra charge subject to availability. Date change requests made less than 72 hours before your scheduled date may be treated as a cancellation.

6. On the Day

Duration: Your companion experience is approximately 8 hours. The start time is flexible and will be agreed upon in advance.

Itinerary: The itinerary is flexible. You set the pace. Your companion will adapt the day based on weather, energy levels, and what interests you in the moment.

Transport: Your private car and driver are available for the full duration of the experience. All driving is handled by a licensed, insured driver.

Meals: Your companion and driver's meals are on us. Your meals and any snacks or drinks are not included in the service price.

We want everyone to have a great day. Shanghai Unlocked reserves the right to end the service early without refund if a client's behavior is abusive, threatening, or otherwise makes the companion or driver unsafe. This is rare, but we have a duty of care to our team.
