Plan Your Day

Curated routes, day trips, or build from scratch. Your Shanghai, your pace.

Three curated day plans. Pick one that matches your vibe — or swap any stop with something from the à la carte menu.

Set A

Classic Shanghai

Best for first-timers. The essential Shanghai, from tree-lined lanes to ancient gardens to the skyline.

  • 9:00 – 12:30French Concession Walk — Jing'an Temple, Wukang Road, Anfu Road, Julu/Fumin/Changle3.5 hrs
  • 12:30 – 13:30Lunch near Jing'an / French Concession1 hr
  • 13:30 – 15:30Yu Garden (豫园) — Ming Dynasty garden, rockeries, pavilions, bazaar2 hrs
  • 15:30 – 16:30The Bund (外滩) — iconic skyline walk, timed for golden hour1 hr
  • 17:00Drop-off at your dinner spot

Walking level: High. On Mondays Yu Garden is closed — we will swap in Shanghai Museum or Shanghai Tower.

Set B

Chinese Heritage

For culture lovers. Temples, propaganda art, and a 1,700-year-old pagoda next to contemporary galleries.

  • 9:00 – 10:30Jade Buddha Temple (玉佛寺) — jade Buddha statues from Myanmar1.5 hrs
  • 10:50 – 11:50Propaganda Poster Art Centre — original posters, 1920s to 1990s1 hr
  • 12:00 – 13:00Lunch near Changning / Xuhui area1 hr
  • 13:30 – 17:00Longhua Temple + West Bund — ancient pagoda + contemporary art + riverside3.5 hrs
  • 17:30Drop-off at your dinner spot

Walking level: Medium. Propaganda Poster Art Centre is on the 7th floor of an office building — we know the way.

Set C

Easy & Comfort

Museum, design district, and the Bund — all at a relaxed pace. Great for rainy days, families, or less walking.

  • 9:00 – 11:00Shanghai Museum (上海博物馆) — world-class Chinese art, free entry, indoors2 hrs
  • 11:30 – 13:00Columbia Circle (上生新所) + Lunch — 1920s expat club, design district1.5 hrs
  • 13:30 – 14:30Nanjing Road West (南京西路) — historic architecture1 hr
  • 14:30 – 15:30The Bund (外滩) — skyline, river breeze, photos1 hr
  • 16:00Optional: afternoon tea at Yuangu (元古) or Peninsula Hotel

Walking level: Low. If you would rather sip tea in a courtyard than pound the pavement, this is your set.

Panlong Tiandi 蟠龙天地

~8 hours · ~1 hr drive · Free entry

A 1,400-year-old water town reborn. Canals, rowing boats, contemporary art, riverside dining. Metro-accessible.

Zhujiajiao 朱家角

~8 hours · ~1 hr drive · Boat rides extra

The classic Jiangnan water town. Stone bridges, narrow lanes, boat rides through ancient canals.

Suzhou 苏州

~10 hours · ~1.5 hr drive · Garden tickets extra

UNESCO gardens, Grand Canal, silk culture. A full-day immersion in classical China.
